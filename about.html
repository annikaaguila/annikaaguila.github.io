About

Hi, I'm Annika.

I make things that move — on screen, in a vase, and on the page.

I work in marketing and social media, with a background as a director, cinematographer, and editor. I'm drawn to stories that sit somewhere between the personal and the observed — narrative shorts, PSAs, and brand films built from the ground up: shot list, camera, cut, and sound.

Outside the edit bay, I work across floral design, photography, and creative writing — different tools, same instinct: notice what's worth framing, then frame it well.

Marketing + Social Media Direction Cinematography Editing Sound Design Photography Floral Design

Behind the Camera

Seven films in, my instinct is still the same one I started with: find the real moment and hold the frame until it means something — whether that's a narrative short, a PSA, or a brand film built from shot list to final mix.

Off Camera

When I'm not in the edit bay, I'm usually arranging flowers, shooting stills on the street, or filling a notebook. Same eye, different tools — you can see some of that in the Photography section.
